Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Café Franca uses cookies on this website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work properly and can remember your preferences during your visit.

2. Cookies we use

Café Franca uses only strictly necessary cookies that are required for the website to function correctly. These do not track you and do not require consent under the GDPR.

  • Session cookies to keep the website secure and working
  • Preference cookies that remember basic settings during your visit

We do not use advertising, marketing or third-party tracking cookies. If we introduce analytics in the future, we will ask for your consent first.

3. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the website works.

4. Contact

Questions about our use of cookies? Contact us at hello@cafefranca.de.
