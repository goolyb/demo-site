Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

At Café Franca we care about your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data when you visit our website or get in touch with us. We process your data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is:

Café Franca

Email: hello@cafefranca.de

Phone: +49 89 4152 7836

2. Data we collect

Contact form

When you use our contact form we collect the name, email address and message you provide. We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry.

Server log data

When you visit our website, your browser automatically transmits data such as your IP address, browser type, the date and time of the request and the page visited. This data is used to ensure a secure and stable operation of our website.

3. Legal basis for processing

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — your consent, e.g. when you submit the contact form.
  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — processing necessary to respond to your request.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in the secure and efficient operation of our website.

4. Cookies

Our website uses only technically necessary cookies required for the website to function. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies without your prior consent.

5. Data retention

We store your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by statutory retention periods. Once the purpose no longer applies, your data is deleted.

6. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with service providers (e.g. hosting) who process it on our behalf under a data processing agreement, or where we are legally obliged to do so.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Request correction of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Request erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@cafefranca.de.
